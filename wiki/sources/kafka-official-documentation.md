## Что изучено

- Broker internals: storage, replication, controller metadata, KRaft quorum.
- Client semantics: producer idempotence, transactions, consumer group protocol, offsets.
- Topic lifecycle: partitioning strategy, retention, compaction, cleanup behavior.
- Operations: metrics, lag, rebalance tuning, rolling restart practices.
- Ecosystem: Kafka Connect and schema integration patterns.
